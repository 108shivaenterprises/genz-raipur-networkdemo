# V124 — Room login se commission split tak aankh se dekha (10 Nov)

Pichhli baar do hisse chhoot gaye the; ab demo QR se poora chalaya (phone size):

1. **Customer ka room:** chat ke card se room khola → mobile daala → code (demo me broker ki screen par dikhta hai) → room ke andar: broker ka naam + RERA, Call / WhatsApp, chuna hua ghar (पसंद / नहीं, विज़िट माँगें), kagaz bhejna, aur broker se chat. Bot ki puraani baatcheet room me maujood thi. Theek chala.
2. **Deal done:** broker ne lead par "Deal done" dabaya → apni brokerage ₹3,00,000 likhi → app ne dukaan ka hissa **₹60,000 (20%)** nikala, "Partner's share due" dikhaya, "I have paid" / "Correct the share" button.
3. **Dukaandaar ka page:** "आपका हिस्सा बकाया है ₹60,000" + "✔ ठीक है" / "सही नहीं है" / "पूछें"; ginti: 1 lead · 1 deal · ₹60,000 baaki.

Koi error nahi, hisaab sahi.

## Jo galat mila aur theek kiya
- Deal ho jaane ke baad bhi lead card par brief **"Cold · 0 — Next: Nothing to do"** dikhta tha, jabki dukaan ka hissa dena baaki tha. Ab poori hui (ya band) lead par brief nahi dikhta; wahan "Deal done · share due" wala box hi rehta hai.

## Jo nahi dekha
- "I have paid" ke baad dukaandaar ka "paisa mila" confirm.
- Do broker wali deal (deal room) ka commission split aur GENZ fee: ye alag screen hai (Deals / Hisaab), is baar nahi chalayi; tests se chalta hai.
- Asli SMS: demo me code broker ki screen par aata hai.
