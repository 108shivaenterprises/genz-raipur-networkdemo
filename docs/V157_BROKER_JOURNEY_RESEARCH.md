## 2. Case studies (web se; ye topi ke tukde hain, poore verify nahi)
1. **Bait-and-switch listing** (Quora, Reddit r/indianrealestate): sasta nakli ghar daalo → bahut phone aate hain → "wo to kal bik gaya, doosra dikhaata hu". Chhote broker ke liye ye lead lene ka tareeka ban gaya; portal bhi rokte nahi kyunki broker unki kamai hain.
2. **WhatsApp group ka shor** (Instagram reels, broker-to-broker apps ke ad): har broker 40 groups me; aadhi listing purani; best co-broking deal muted group me padi rehti hai.
3. **Portal cloning / token scam** (reel, LinkedIn post): asli listing copy karke nakli site; token transfer karwa lete hain. Token se pehle owner ki pehchaan zaroori.
4. **Broker se nafrat ke kaaran** (Facebook post): jhoothe vaade, baar-baar call, chhupa commission, galat jaankari. Jo broker saaf likhit hissa aur record dikhaye, wo alag dikhta hai.
5. **Registry ka raasta** (bookpropertyvisit, sansalegal, drawmagic): kaagzaat jaanch → agreement to sell (token/bayana, tareekh) → stamp duty + registration fees → sale deed → sub-registrar me dono + 2 gawah ID/photo/biometric ke saath → dakhil-kharij. Registration-day checklist: kya lekar jaana, TDS, stamp.

Seekh: lead tab jaati hai jab jawab der se aaye; deal tab latakti hai jab **tareekh aur likhit tay cheezen** na hon; bharosa tab banta hai jab har kadam ka record ho.

## 4. Banane ka plan (kam mehnat, zyada asar pehle)
A. **Deal ki tareekhein**: token, agreement, registry, possession; deal me ek patti "Registry 12 din me"; dono broker ko reminder; registry-din ki "kya lekar jaana" list; registry ke baad dakhil-kharij ka kaam.
B. **Saath baithak (visit slot)**: broker samay prastaavit kare → owner aur client dono "haan" kahein tab pakka → reminder.
C. **Insta / WhatsApp post maker**: listing se caption + hashtag + group ke liye chhota text, ek tap copy (photo / reel ke saath).
D. **Zaroorat par jawab ki daud**: "mere paas hai" ek tap, kitni der me jawab aaya dikhe, chup rehne wale ko yaad.
