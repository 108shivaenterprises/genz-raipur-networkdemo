## Chaar flow (ek dukaan ke QR se, phone size, broker ki sharten: bikri 20%, kiraya ₹2,000)
| Flow | Customer ki taraf | Broker ki taraf | Deal done → dukaan ka hissa |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Khareedna** | Form → chat → ✅ → ghar dikha, number khula, room card, "कब तक?" | Brief Warm/Hot + agla kadam, "Go to his client room" | Brokerage ₹30,000 → **₹6,000 (20%)** ✔ |
| **Kiraye par lena** | Form → chat → ✅ → koi kiraye ka ghar nahi tha: bot ne saaf bataya, room card phir bhi mila, "ब्रोकर से बात" | Number chhupa (niyam), brief: "Nothing fits — Radar" | Brokerage ₹30,000 → **₹2,000 (fix)** ✔ |
| **Bechna** | Form (shart nahi maangi) → ✅ → bot ne 8 sawal puchhe (prakar, BHK, size, manzil, ilaka, kab khaali, daam, kagaz) → "ड्राफ़्ट ब्रोकर के पास" | Number khula, listing ka draft | Brokerage ₹2,00,000 → **₹40,000 (20%)** ✔ |
| **Kiraye par dena** | Wahi + furnishing ka sawal → draft | Wahi | Brokerage ₹2,00,000 → **₹2,000 (fix)** ✔ |

Dukaandaar ke page par chaaron hisse alag card me, kul "4 lead · 4 deal · ₹50,000 baaki". Kahin error nahi.

## Jo galat mila aur theek kiya
- Dukaandaar ko **pehli baar** hissa milne par bhi likha aata tha "⚠ आपका हिस्सा **बदला** है". Ab pehli baar "💰 आपका हिस्सा **तय हुआ**"; "बदला है" sirf tab jab sach me badle.

## Jo is baar bhi aankh se nahi dekha
- Bechne / kiraye par dene wale ka **draft → asli listing → owner ki manzoori ka link**.
- "I have paid" ke baad dukaandaar ka "paisa mila".
- **Do broker wali deal** (deal room, token, commission split, GENZ fee) aur **rent desk** (kiraya agreement, mahine ka kiraya).
In sabke tests pass hain, par screen par maine nahi chalaya.
