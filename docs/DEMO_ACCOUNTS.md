## What each employee can do

- **Broker / builder employees**: only the leads and deal rooms their employer allots; no commission, split, payout or inventory control.
- **Contractor employees**: only the construction jobs awarded to their employer, without commission or contract value; cannot accept new leads.
- **Admin / Super Admin employees**: Connector Desk — view, assign and progress referrals; no reward approval, payout, settings or KYC decisions.
