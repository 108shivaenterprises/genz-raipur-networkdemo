# v68 — Magic Match: apne ghar + apne buyer par co-broking nahi

## Kya tha
Magic Match har match par "Co-broking offer" dikhata tha, chahe ghar aapki hi listing ho aur buyer bhi aapka. Apne aap se co-broking ka matlab nahi (server bhi "yourself" par rok deta hai).

## Ab
- Jahan **ghar bhi aapka aur buyer need bhi aapka** (dono taraf se): button badalkar **"आपका अपना घर और खरीदार — क्लाइंट रूम खोलें"**. Dabane par naya room form khulta hai: buyer ka naam/number bhara, ghar room me apne aap judne wala (v67).
- Jahan ek taraf doosre broker ka hai: pehle jaisa **Co-broking offer** (sahi case).
- "For a buyer" aur "For a property", dono mode me.

Files: `MatchCenterView.tsx`, `App.tsx` (meId). Server me badlav nahi.
