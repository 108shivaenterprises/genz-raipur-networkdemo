## 2. Jo jaan-boojh kar NAHI hataya (aapko pata hona chahiye)
- **Due-diligence / paper-check report ka notice** ("ye platform ki andar ki jaanch hai, adalati praman-patra nahi…"). Ye "vakil se pucho" wali line nahi hai; ye batata hai ki GENZ ka "verified" kya hai aur kya nahi. Ise hatane par koi khareedar kharab title par GENZ ko zimmedar thehra sakta hai. Bolo to iski bhasha aur saaf / chhoti kar dun.
- "You pay the shop yourself; GENZ only records it" aur "GENZ does not hold or move this money": ye tathya hain, bachav nahi.
- **Sach:** Terms / Privacy pages ka text abhi bhi ek template hi hai. Banner hatane se wo kisi vakil ka dekha hua nahi ho jaata. Asli launch se pehle ek baar dikhwa lena aapke hi fayde me hai; app ab ye kisi ko nahi batata.
