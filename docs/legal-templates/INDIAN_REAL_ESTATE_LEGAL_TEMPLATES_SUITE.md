## 2. 📝 Template 1: Resale Agreement to Sell (Residential Flat / House)
*Applicable for: 2 BHK / 3 BHK Flats, Independent Bungalows, Villas across all states.*

```text
================================================================================
                    AGREEMENT TO SELL / विक्रय इकरारनामा
================================================================================
(Leave top 4.5 inches blank for physical or e-Stamp Paper printing)
(Pursuant to Section 54, Transfer of Property Act, 1882 & Indian Contract Act, 1872)

This Agreement to Sell is made and executed on this _____ day of ____________, 20___ at {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.

BETWEEN:

{{SELLER_NAME}}, Son/Daughter/Wife of {{SELLER_FATHER_NAME}}, aged about _____ years, 
residing at {{SELLER_ADDRESS}}, PAN: {{SELLER_PAN}}, Aadhaar: {{SELLER_AADHAAR}}, 
Mobile: {{SELLER_PHONE}}
(hereinafter called the "SELLER / FIRST PARTY", which expression shall include legal heirs, successors, and assigns) of the FIRST PART.

AND:

{{BUYER_NAME}}, Son/Daughter/Wife of {{BUYER_FATHER_NAME}}, aged about _____ years, 
residing at {{BUYER_ADDRESS}}, PAN: {{BUYER_PAN}}, Aadhaar: {{BUYER_AADHAAR}}, 
Mobile: {{BUYER_PHONE}}
(hereinafter called the "BUYER / SECOND PARTY", which expression shall include legal heirs, successors, and assigns) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS:
1. The Seller is the sole, absolute, and undisputed owner of the immovable property bearing {{PROPERTY_TYPE}}:
   - Property Address: {{PROPERTY_FULL_ADDRESS}}
   - {{STATE_LAND_IDENTIFIER}}: {{LAND_RECORD_NUMBER}} (e.g. Khasra No. / CTS No. / 7-12 / Unit No.)
   - Super Built-up Area: {{SUPER_AREA}} Sq.Ft. | Carpet Area: {{CARPET_AREA}} Sq.Ft.
2. The Seller acquired title via registered deed bearing Document No. {{PRIOR_DEED_NO}} dated {{PRIOR_DEED_DATE}} at Sub-Registrar Office {{SUB_REGISTRAR_OFFICE}}.
3. The Seller has agreed to sell and the Buyer has agreed to purchase the said property for a total sale consideration of ₹{{TOTAL_SALE_PRICE}}/- (Rupees {{TOTAL_SALE_PRICE_WORDS}} only).

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. TOTAL CONSIDERATION & EARNEST MONEY (BAYANA):
   - Total Agreed Price: ₹{{TOTAL_SALE_PRICE}}/-.
   - Earnest Money / Bayana Received: ₹{{BAYANA_AMOUNT}}/- paid vide {{PAYMENT_MODE_DETAILS}} dated {{PAYMENT_DATE}}, receipt of which Seller hereby acknowledges.
   - Balance Amount: ₹{{BALANCE_AMOUNT}}/- to be paid at the time of final registry.

2. TIMELINE FOR REGISTRATION:
   The final Sale Deed shall be registered before the Sub-Registrar on or before {{REGISTRY_DEADLINE_DATE}} (within {{DAYS_ALLOWED}} days from execution).

3. CLEAR & ENCUMBRANCE-FREE TITLE:
   The Seller covenants that the property is completely free from all mortgages, bank loans, liens, court attachments, family disputes, and municipal tax arrears. All utility bills (electricity, water, maintenance) shall be cleared by the Seller up to the date of registry.

4. 1% TDS STATUTORY DEDUCTION (SECTION 194-IA):
   If the total sale price is ₹50,00,000/- or higher, the Buyer shall deduct 1% TDS from the sale consideration and deposit it with the Government of India via Form 26QB, providing the TDS certificate (Form 16B) to the Seller.

5. DEFAULT & FORFEITURE CLAUSES:
   a) Buyer's Default: If the Buyer fails to pay the balance consideration by {{REGISTRY_DEADLINE_DATE}}, the Seller shall be entitled to forfeit the earnest money (up to 10% of total price).
   b) Seller's Default: If the Seller refuses to execute the sale deed after receiving bayana, the Buyer shall be entitled to full refund plus an equivalent penalty amount, or file a suit for Specific Performance under the Specific Relief Act, 1963.

6. POSSESSION:
   Vacant, peaceful, physical possession of the property shall be handed over to the Buyer simultaneously with full payment at the Sub-Registrar office.

SCHEDULE OF THE PROPERTY:
- North: {{BOUND_NORTH}}
- South: {{BOUND_SOUTH}}
- East:  {{BOUND_EAST}}
- West:  {{BOUND_WEST}}

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement in presence of the witnesses.

___________________________                     ___________________________
SELLER / FIRST PARTY                            BUYER / SECOND PARTY

Witness 1: _____________________                Witness 2: _____________________
Name & Phone:                                   Name & Phone:
```

## 3. 🌾 Template 2: Plot & Land Sale Agreement
*Applicable for: Residential Layout Plots, Commercial Plots, Agricultural/Diversion Land.*

```text
================================================================================
              PLOT & LAND SALE AGREEMENT / भूखंड विक्रय अनुबंध
================================================================================
(Includes State-Specific Land Revenue Clauses for CG/MP/MH/UP/KA)

Execution Date: _____ / _____ / 20___ at {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.

Between: {{SELLER_NAME}} (Seller) and {{BUYER_NAME}} (Buyer).

1. LAND DESCRIPTION & REVENUE RECORDS:
   - Plot No.: {{PLOT_NUMBER}}, Layout Name: {{LAYOUT_NAME}}
   - {{STATE_LAND_LABEL}}: {{KHASRA_OR_SURVEY_NO}}, Patwari Halka / Ward: {{PH_NO}}
   - Total Plot Area: {{PLOT_AREA_SQFT}} Sq.Ft. ({{PLOT_AREA_SQYARDS}} Sq.Yards)
   - Diversion Approval Order No.: {{DIVERSION_ORDER_NO}} (Residential / Commercial / Industrial).
   - Town & Country Planning (T&CP) / Colonizer Approval: {{TCP_APPROVAL_NO}}.
   - RERA Project Registration (if colony): {{RERA_REG_NO}}.

2. BOUNDARY PEGGING & DEMARCATION (सीमांकन):
   The Seller guarantees that the exact corner boundaries of the plot are physically demarcated on site. The Seller shall arrange for revenue demarcation (Seemankan) through the Revenue Inspector / Patwari prior to registry if required by the Buyer.

3. ACCESS & ROAD CONNECTIVITY:
   The Seller assures that the plot has an unobstructed direct approach through a {{ROAD_WIDTH_FEET}} ft wide public road as sanctioned in the master layout plan.

4. CONSIDERATION, BAYANA & PAYMENT MILESTONES:
   - Total Rate: ₹{{RATE_PER_SQFT}} per Sq.Ft. = Total ₹{{TOTAL_PLOT_PRICE}}/-.
   - Advance Bayana: ₹{{BAYANA_AMOUNT}}/-.
   - Balance Amount: ₹{{BALANCE_AMOUNT}}/- payable upon final deed registration.

5. AGRICULTURAL RESTRICTION WARRANTY (For Rural / Non-Diversified Land):
   Seller confirms the land does not belong to protected tribal categories under Section 165/170-B (in CG/MP Land Revenue Code) or equivalent state restrictions, and has unencumbered alienable title.

SELLER SIGNATURE: _______________________    BUYER SIGNATURE: _______________________
Witness 1: ______________________________    Witness 2: ______________________________
```

## 4. 🏠 Template 3: 11-Month Residential Leave & License
*Applicable for: Residential Flats, Independent Floors, House Rentals.*

```text
================================================================================
                   LEAVE & LICENSE AGREEMENT (RESIDENTIAL)
================================================================================
(Standard 11-Month Non-Tenancy Agreement)

Between: {{OWNER_NAME}} (Licensor/Landlord) and {{TENANT_NAME}} (Licensee/Tenant).

Premises: Flat/House No. {{UNIT_NO}}, {{BUILDING_NAME}}, {{STREET}}, {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.

1. DURATION:
   Fixed period of 11 (eleven) months commencing on {{START_DATE}} and concluding on {{END_DATE}}.

2. MONTHLY COMPENSATION (RENT):
   ₹{{MONTHLY_RENT}}/- per month, payable in advance on or before the {{DUE_DAY}} of each English calendar month via Bank Transfer / UPI: {{OWNER_UPI_OR_BANK}}.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
   ₹{{SECURITY_DEPOSIT}}/- paid by Licensee to Licensor. Fully refundable at vacation after adjusting unpaid electricity/water charges or physical structural damages beyond normal wear and tear.

4. NO TENANCY / OCCUPANCY RIGHTS:
   The Licensee is granted purely permissive temporary occupancy under the Indian Easements Act, 1882 {{MAHARASHTRA_S55_CLAUSE_IF_APPLICABLE}}. No tenancy, leasehold or permanent protection rights are created.

5. POLICE VERIFICATION & SOCIETY COMPLIANCE:
   The Licensee shall submit all required KYC documents for local Police Verification. The Licensee shall strictly follow society by-laws and refrain from commercial or illegal activities.

6. NOTICE PERIOD:
   Either party may terminate by serving 30 (thirty) days' written notice.

LICENSOR: ___________________________       LICENSEE: ___________________________
```

```text
================================================================================
                     COMMERCIAL LEASE / LICENSE DEED
================================================================================
Between: {{LESSOR_COMPANY_OR_OWNER}} (Lessor) and {{LESSEE_COMPANY_OR_TENANT}} (Lessee).

Property: Commercial Unit No. {{SHOP_OR_OFFICE_NO}}, {{COMPLEX_NAME}}, {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.

1. COMMERCIAL PURPOSE:
   Strictly for operating {{BUSINESS_ACTIVITY_TYPE}} complying with municipal shop & establishment laws.

2. LEASE TERM & LOCK-IN PERIOD:
   - Total Lease Period: {{LEASE_YEARS}} Years.
   - Mandatory Lock-in Period: {{LOCK_IN_MONTHS}} Months (Neither party may terminate during lock-in without paying rent for remainder of lock-in period).

3. RENT ESCALATION (किराया वृद्धि):
   Monthly Rent: ₹{{BASE_COMMERCIAL_RENT}}/- + Applicable GST.
   Rent shall escalate by {{ESCALATION_PERCENT}}% (Standard 5% annually or 15% every 3 years).

4. GST & STATUTORY DEDUCTIONS (TDS 194-I):
   - If Lessee is a GST-registered business, rent shall be invoiced with 18% GST.
   - Lessee shall deduct 10% TDS under Section 194-I (or 2% for machinery) and furnish Form 16A quarterly.

5. FIT-OUT PERIOD & ALTERATIONS:
   Lessee is granted {{FITOUT_DAYS}} days rent-free fit-out period for interior setup without altering core structural pillars.

LESSOR: _____________________________       LESSEE: _____________________________
```

## 6. 🤝 Template 5: Exclusive Broker Mandate & Anti-Bypass Agreement
*Protects the Broker's 1-2% Commission and Introductions under RERA.*

```text
================================================================================
         REAL ESTATE BROKER COMMISSION MANDATE & NON-BYPASS UNDERTAKING
================================================================================

This Mandate is executed at {{CITY}}, {{STATE}} on _____ / _____ / 20___

BETWEEN:
{{OWNER_OR_BUYER_NAME}} (hereinafter the "PRINCIPAL")
AND
{{BROKER_NAME}} / {{BROKER_AGENCY_NAME}} (hereinafter the "BROKER")
RERA Agent Registration Number: {{BROKER_RERA_REG_NO}}

1. MANDATE & SCOPE:
   The Principal authorizes the Broker to market and negotiate the sale/purchase/lease of {{PROPERTY_NAME_OR_ADDRESS}}.

2. AGREED COMMISSION RATE:
   - Sale/Purchase: {{BROKERAGE_PERCENT}}% of the total registered transaction value.
   - Rental/Lease: {{RENT_COMMISSION_MONTHS}} month(s) rent.
   Payable immediately upon execution of the Agreement to Sell or Registration of Sale Deed.

3. STRICT NON-BYPASS & ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION:
   a) Any buyer, seller, or tenant introduced by the Broker (via WhatsApp, platform log, SMS, or physical site visit pass) is a protected client of the Broker.
   b) The Principal covenants NOT to circumvent, bypass, or directly close any deal with the introduced party to evade commission.
   c) 180-Day Tail / Protection Period: If the Principal closes a deal with any introduced party within 180 days after this mandate expires, full commission remains legally payable.
   d) Breach Penalty: If bypass occurs, the Principal is liable to pay full brokerage PLUS 50% liquidated damages and recovery legal fees under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

PRINCIPAL SIGNATURE: _______________________    BROKER SIGNATURE: _______________________
```
