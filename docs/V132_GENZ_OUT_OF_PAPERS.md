# V132 — GENZ kagaz ki jaanch ka daava nahi karta (12 Nov)

## Aapki baat
"GENZ ko kagaz me mat fasao; kal fraud hua to company par aayega."

## Kya galat tha
App kai jagah **"verified" property / ghar / listing** likhta tha, jabki broker ki listing dalte hi "verified" ho jaati thi: GENZ ne koi kagaz dekha hi nahi hota. Yaani daava bhi tha aur uske peeche jaanch bhi nahi. Ye sabse bada jokhim tha.

## Ab GENZ ki jagah
- **Kagaz kaun dalta / jaanchta hai:** broker, owner, vakil. App me naam aur tareekh ke saath record rehta hai.
- **GENZ kya karta hai:** record rakhta hai, kaun-kya-kab dikhata hai. Title ya kagaz ka praman nahi deta.
- Title-check ka tool (6 charan, 30 saal ki khoj, khasra) waise hi hai; wo broker / vakil ka kaam hai, GENZ ka daava nahi.

## Jo nahi badla (aapka faisla chahiye)
- **"GENZ Verified Broker" / "KYC — GENZ ने जाँचा"** broker ke page aur QR par. Ye insaan ki pehchaan (PAN / Aadhaar) ki baat hai, property ki nahi, aur GENZ sach me ID dekhta hai. Par ye bhi ek daava hai: koi "verified" broker dhokha de to sawal GENZ par aa sakta hai. Vikalp: "ID checked" / "KYC done".
- Andar ka status naam `VERIFIED` (code me) waisa hi hai; user ko ab "live" dikhta hai.
