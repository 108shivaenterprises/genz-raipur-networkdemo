# V142–V144 — Halka Deals aur Deal Room (broker, phone)

Aapka kehna: "bahot heavy hai, halka karo, dekh ke insaan ghabra jayega", aur "jo broker use nahi karte wo hata do, doosre apps se layout ka reference lo".

## Reference (layout kahan se liya)
- **Pipedrive** ka deal detail: upar chhota header + stage ki patti, phir "Focus" (agla kaam), baaki sab neeche. Har deal ke saath hamesha ek agla kaam juda rehta hai.
- **Zoho Bigin** ka real-estate pipeline: stage-wise seedhi list, mobile par ek deal = ek card.
- In dono se liya: (1) deal kholte hi ek hi agla kadam, (2) section kholne par sirf wahi section, wapas ka ek teer, (3) kam number, bade button.

## Kya badla (sirf phone; bade screen par sab pehle jaisa)
### Deals ki list
- "Deal room" tab hata (room deal dabane se khulta hai).
- 5 ya kam deal hon to search / filter ki line nahi dikhti.
- Card par "7 duties to finish" ki jagah agle kaam ka naam.

### Deal room (deal dabate hi)
- Upar sirf "← All deals". Title, tabs ki patti, deal ka dropdown hata.
- Card: ghar ka naam, ilaka, stage, **ek agla kadam**. Deal code / All IDs / Team line hati.
- Teen number ek patti me: mera hissa, deal ki keemat, doosra broker.
- Chaar bade button: Chat, Tasks, Agreement, Money. Baaki (Photos & papers, Paper check, Full deal room) "More" me.
- "View: Simple / Full" switch hata.

### Section ke andar (Tasks, Agreement, Chat…)
- Sirf wahi section dikhta hai. Upar "← ghar ka naam" se room par wapas. Pehle har section ke upar poora card + agla kadam + switch + tabs dobara aate the (lagbhag aadhi screen).
- Tasks: 4 number ki jagah "13 me se 1 kaam poora" + patti. "Complete Duty" → "Mark done".
- Agreement: SHA-256 hash, USR-BRK code, TDS / GST wali line phone par nahi. "Entitlement & payout record" → "Who gets what", "Entitlement" → "Share".
- Overview: broker ke naam ke neeche GZ-BRK code phone par nahi.

## Jo nahi hataya (aapka faisla)
- Deals me "Offers / Client rooms / Owners" tabs, jo More → Deal Desk me bhi hain (do jagah).
- Paper check (due diligence) aur Photos & papers ki screens andar se abhi bhi bhaari hain; wo "More" ke peeche hain.
- Agreement me "Propose Amendment (V2)" aur "Download Record".
