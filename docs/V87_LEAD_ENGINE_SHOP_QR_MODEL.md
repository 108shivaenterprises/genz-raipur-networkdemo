# v87 — Lead engine: broker ke liye sabse zyada client requirement + owner listing, shop QR hamara MSP

Yeh MODEL hai (plan). Is doc mein koi code nahi badla. Screenshots (94 + shop QR/dukaan page/Partners tab) dekh kar likha gaya.

## 1) Screenshots mein kya dikha (sach)
**Achha:** broker Home saaf hai (WhatsApp se listing/need, Client's need, Magic Match). Shop QR customer page do bade button deta hai: "मुझे घर चाहिए" / "मुझे अपनी प्रॉपर्टी देनी है", aur broker ke ghar dikhata hai.

**Kamzori (lead badhane ke nazariye se):**
1. **Shop QR broker ke Home par hai hi nahi.** Wo More → Sales & Team mein chhupa hai. Jo hamara sabse bada growth tool hai, wo teen tap door hai. Home par "Shop QR" ka tile nahi, "kitne scan / lead aaye" nahi.
2. **Partners tab par lead card bahut bhari** hai: har card par wahi lambi chetavni ("A call or WhatsApp is not recorded in GENZ…") dohrai gayi hai. Kaam ke button (Open client room, Find in network) dab jaate hain. Naya broker thak jaata hai.
3. **Dukaan ke page par pehli cheez "Alerts blocked" ka bada hara dabba** hai (jab permission na ho). Dukaandar ko pehle "aapne itna kamaya / itne scan hue" dikhna chahiye, alert baad mein.
4. **Dukaandar ko motivation kam:** scan/lead/deal ginti hai, par "agar 5 lead deal bani to ₹X" jaisa andaaza, ya kya karna hai (poster kahan lagayein), nahi dikhta. Rank tabhi dikhta hai jab 3+ dukaan hon.
5. **Customer ko form ke baad kuch milta nahi** jo wo share kare (jaise "mera ghar dhoondhne ka status"). Ek customer 1 hi baar lead deta hai.
6. **Owner ki listing ke liye alag raasta nahi.** QR par "मुझे अपनी प्रॉपर्टी देनी है" hai par broker ko owner lead ka alag follow-up kaam nahi dikhta.
7. **Broker ko target nahi:** "is hafte 3 requirement + 2 owner listing" jaisa koi chhota lakshya Home par nahi.

## 2) Model: ek hi funnel, teen sources
Broker ka paisa = (kitni **requirement** apne paas) × (kitni **supply/listing** apne paas) match hone se. Isliye har kaam ka ek hi maqsad: dono taraf zyada.

| Source | Kya milta hai | Hamari takat |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Shop QR (MSP)** | Khareedar/kirayedar aur owner dono, har mohalle ki dukaan se | Dukaan ka bharosa + roz ka foot-traffic, broker ko CAC ~0 |
| **B. Broker ka apna public page + WhatsApp share** | Apne ghar dikha ke requirement | Pehle se hai (v57) |
| **C. Network (doosre brokers)** | Match, co-broking | Pehle se hai (Magic Match, Buyer Needs) |

**Shop QR kyun MSP:** ek dukaan = ek mohalla. Dawai ki dukaan, kirana, salon, society gate, photocopy, ISP/cable wale — wahan roz sainkdon log aate hain, aur wahan se "ghar chahiye / kiraya chahiye / bechna hai" ka sawaal sabse sasta milta hai. Propi/NoBroker online ads par kharch karte hain; hamara channel ground par hai aur dukaan ko sirf tabhi dena padta hai jab deal ho. Isse naye shehar mein expansion ka formula banta hai: **1 shehar = 1 sthaniya broker + 50–100 dukaan.**

## 3) Kya banana hai (Impact ÷ mehnat ke hisaab se, pehle sabse upar)
**Chhota, turant (1–2 din):**
1. **Home par "Shop QR" tile**: "इस हफ़्ते: N scan · N leads · N naye owner" aur ek button "नई दुकान जोड़ें". Agar 0 partner hon to bada call-to-action "पहली दुकान जोड़ें (2 मिनट)".
2. **Partners tab saaf karna**: lambi chetavni ek hi baar upar (ya "i" icon), har card mein sirf naam · ilaka · need · status · 2 bade button (Open client room, Find in network).
3. **Dukaan ka page naya order**: kamai/ginti pehle, "kaise zyada lead laayein" ka chhota card (3 tips + poster), alert ka card neeche.
4. **Lead ke 3 baar follow-up kaam** Home ke to-do mein: "Sanjay Verma ko 2 ghante mein reply karo" (pehla reply sabse badi conversion deta hai).

**Madhyam (1 hafta):**
5. **Dukaan ke liye "kamai ka andaaza"**: page par "agar aapki 5 leads deal banin to ~₹X" (broker ke tay hissa se auto; deal ka price nahi dikhana). Isse dukaan ko lagta hai ki mehnat ka matlab hai.
6. **Customer ko form ke baad kuch dena**: chat mein turant "aapke liye 3 ghar" (match wale ghar). Isse wo wapas aata hai, aur share karta hai.
7. **Owner-lead ka alag raasta**: QR par "मेरी प्रॉपर्टी" → chat mein 3 sawal (kya, kahan, kitna) → broker ke paas "Owner listing" kaam, jisme listing banane ka button seedha prefill ke saath.
8. **Weekly target + streak** broker ke Home par: "2 owner listing, 3 requirement is hafte" ek progress bar; poora hone par leaderboard point (v74 leaderboard pehle se hai).

**Bada (2–4 hafte):**
9. **Dukaan ke liye "QR kit"**: poster ke saath table-tent, sticker, WhatsApp status image — print/PDF. Alag-alag dukaan type (dawai, kirana, salon, society) ke liye alag poster line.
10. **Area-wise dukaan map + khali mohalle**: broker ko dikhe "Shankar Nagar mein 3 dukaan, Pandri mein 0 — yahan lagao".
11. **Referral chain**: ek dukaandar doosre dukaandar ko laaye to chhota bonus (yeh sirf tabhi jab farzi lead ka rasta band ho — v82 mein aapne "bonus/thank-you" band kiya tha, isliye ise aap hi tay karein).
12. **WhatsApp se alert/forward** (API chahiye, baad mein).

## 4) Kamai ka model (suggestion, aap tay karein)
- Abhi: dukaan ko broker ki brokerage ka hissa. Is par koi badlav nahi.
- Agla: broker ke liye **monthly plan** jo "kitne shop partner aur kitni lead" par badhe; builder ke liye project promotion; deal ke baad loan/legal par referral. (Square Yards/NoBroker ka bada hissa yahi hai.)

## 5) Jo galat ho sakta hai (aur bachav)
- **Farzi lead / spam**: honeypot, timer, rate-limit, ek number din mein 3 baar — pehle se. Naye "customer ko kuch dena" feature mein bhi number chat mein nahi.
- **Dukaan ka bypass** (dukaandar customer se seedha deal kare): QR par sirf broker ka naam; customer ka number dukaan ko nahi.
- **Broker bypass**: v85 se number chhupa hai jab tak need confirm + match na ho.
- **Jyada dukaan = jyada kaam**: broker ek saath 20 dukaan nahi sambhal paayega. Isliye pehle chhota (3 dukaan) sikhao, phir badhao.
- **Kanooni**: dukaan ko "referral fee" kehna aur DLT/SMS — CA/vakil se ek baar.

## 6) Kaise naapein (agar banaya)
Har broker ke liye: dukaan/broker, scan→lead %, lead→reply (2 ghante), lead→visit, lead→deal, ₹/lead. Network ke liye: dukaan jinse pichhle 30 din mein ek bhi lead aayi (active shop %).

## Limits
- Yeh sirf model hai. Koi data real dukaan/broker par test nahi hua.
- Screenshots demo data par hain (photo nahi dikhti kyunki is machine par Unsplash block hai).
- Numbers (scan→lead %) bina real data ke andaaza hi hain; pehle pilot chalana hoga.
